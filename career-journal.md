Which concept was hardest to understand?
Risk was the hardest at first because it combines assets, threats, and vulnerabilities. After studying examples, I understood that risk is the possibility of harm occurring.
Can you explain the difference between a threat and a vulnerability?
Yes. A threat is something that can cause harm, while a vulnerability is a weakness that allows the threat to succeed.
How would these concepts apply to a company in Abu Dhabi?
An Abu Dhabi company has valuable assets such as employee records, customer information, and business systems. Threats include phishing attacks, ransomware, and insider threats. Vulnerabilities include weak passwords, unpatched systems, and poor security awareness.
 GRC analysts identify these risks and recommend controls such as MFA, security policies, employee training, and regular audits.
